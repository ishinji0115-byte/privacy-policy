This extension ("AI Unified Search", "we", "the extension") helps you search across your ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini conversations from one place, and keeps long conversations running smoothly. This page explains exactly what data we handle and why.

What we collect

While you are using ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), or Gemini (gemini.google.com) with this extension installed, the extension reads the text of the messages displayed on the page (both your messages and the AI's replies) in order to build a local search index and to shorten very long conversations for performance.

We do not collect your account credentials, payment details, browsing history on other websites, or any information from any site other than the three listed above.

Where your data is stored

Free plan (default): All indexed conversation text stays 100% on your own device, using Chrome's built-in local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any server. Uninstalling the extension permanently deletes it.
Premium plan (optional, paid): If you choose to enable cross-device sync, an encrypted copy of your indexed data is stored on our cloud infrastructure (Supabase) so it can be retrieved from your other devices. This only happens if you actively turn this feature on.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.
  • We do not use your conversation data to serve personalized ads.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We do not inject ads or third-party scripts into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

Third-party services we use

If you use the optional paid plan, two third-party services are involved:

  • Stripe — processes payment. We do not see or store your card details; see Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — stores your encrypted synced data if cross-device sync is enabled; see Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Permissions this extension requests, and why

  • Access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com — required to read the messages on those pages so we can index and search them.
  • Storage — required to save your search index locally on your device.

Your control over your data

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by removing the extension from Chrome. If you have enabled the paid sync feature and want your cloud-stored data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country).
